Strategic Hypothesis

Origin Context: The Men's Legacy Brand Expansion

Unpacking the business narrative behind the customer ID split and promotional legacy.

Why are 100% of subscribers and discounts male?

The empirical structure of the database reveals a clear chronological pattern: Customers #1 to #2,652 are all Male, while Customers #2,653 to #3,900 are all Female.

The High-Probability Origin Story:
The brand operated historically as a Specialty Men's Brand / Grooming & Subscription Club (analogous to Dollar Shave Club or Bonobos).

Upon expanding into general retail (introducing Blouses, Dresses, and Skirts), promotional codes and subscription privileges were exclusively grandfathered into the legacy male email database.

The 3 Candidate Legacy Business Models:

1. Men's Grooming & Shaving Subscription Club
Primary model featuring recurring subscription mechanics, expanding into lifestyle apparel.
2. Custom Men's Tailoring & Workwear Brand
Expanded from pants and shirts into women's apparel and accessories.
3. Outdoor, Hunting & Tactical Gear Brand
Historically 90%+ male member base, introducing general retail lines.
